Stand at the classroom door. Greet every student by name. Eye contact. Their name. Every student. Every day without exception.
SAY to each student as they walk in: "Good morning [name]! Free choice at seats is at your seat — go ahead and get started."
⭐ SHEEP TIMER ROUTINE: The timer is already counting down when students arrive. When it goes off — students should already be at bumblebee spots. Not walking there. Already there. If they aren't — walk to your spot and begin without them. Do not wait. Do not announce it. Students who are not on their spot when the meeting starts will not make that mistake twice.

⭐ TEST DAY · Alphablocks review episode first · Assessment after · Celebrate every student
🐑
Sheep timer goes off → students at bumblebee spots → greeting → Activity 1 → Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson.
⭐ MORNING MEETING ORDER — ALWAYS: Sheep timer → bumblebee spots → greeting → PA Activity 1 → PA Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson → brain break → math. Same every day. Students know what comes next. That is the behavior management.
🎵
Activity 1 on bumblebee spots — phonological awareness warm-up — 2 minutes.
SAY: "I am going to say a word. Tell me only the first sound — not the whole word. Just the first sound. Ready? [Say 6-8 words — mostly words starting with today's target sound, 2 distractors with different sounds.] Thumbs up if you hear the TEST DAY · sound at the beginning. Thumbs down if you don't."
🎵
Activity 2 on bumblebee spots — blending and segmenting — 2 minutes.
SAY: "Now I say the sounds — you blend them into a word. Ready? [Say onset and rime or individual phonemes for 4-5 CVC words.] Now reverse — I say the word, you tap out the sounds on your fingers and tell me how many sounds you hear."
📋
Sing to smartboard spots → read morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson.
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!" Song is the signal — not your voice.
MORNING MESSAGE: "Today is Friday. We will review and take our phonics assessment. Our snap word is [to]. Look at our schedule." [Point to every block on the schedule. Name each one out loud.]

Letter sound cards — 3 minutes — every card — every single day — non-negotiable even on test days.

Phonics Lesson — TEST DAY · Module 2 L1-4 · Consonants s, b, n, d + Short i review · Alphablocks + assessment
Alphablocks →
SAY: "We are going to watch our Alphablocks friends one more time — pay attention to every sound you hear. After we watch, I am going to give you a short assessment. This is not a test of how smart you are — it is information for me so I can help you better. Do your best. That is all I ask."
🧰 TOOLBOX — WAIT TIME: After asking a question — wait a full 10 seconds before calling on anyone or answering yourself. Count silently. 10 seconds of silence feels like an eternity but it is the difference between 3 students answering and 22 students thinking.
🧰 TOOLBOX — ASKING FOR HELP: If a student is stuck, they say: "I don't understand how to ____." Not "I don't get it." Redirect every vague request to specific language before helping. Every time.

Snap Word — "to" — introduce and add to the snap word wall.
SAY: [Snap fingers] "Snap word! The word is TO. Spell it with me — T — O. Write it on your hand in the air. Write it again. One more time. Now — we have a, the, as, to snap words. You know all of them in a snap."
Add to snap word wall. Count the words on the wall together.

Math · Unit 3 Lesson 2 · Flat Shapes — Name and describe · count sides and corners · compare shapes
Hook bumblebee spots → mini lesson smartboard spots → seat work with timer → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.G.A.2 K.G.B.4
Objective
Flat Shapes — Name and describe · count sides and corners · compare shapes
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 minutes — spark curiosity — do not teach yet.
SAY: "Hold up a mystery shape covered with a cloth. Pull the cloth off slowly. 'Before I show you — what shape do you think this is? One guess each. Come to smartboard spots and we will find out.'"
🎵 SING to smartboard spots: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson on smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — manipulatives only — original script — no alien videos.
SAY: "Hold up a triangle. 'This is a triangle. How many sides? Count with me: 1, 2, 3. Three sides. How many corners? 1, 2, 3. Three corners. Now — is this triangle different from THIS triangle?' [hold up a different sized triangle] 'Different size — but same shape. Three sides, three corners. That's what makes it a triangle no matter how big or small it is.'"
🧰 TOOLBOX: 8 minutes is the hard cap for whole-group instruction. If you need more time — take it at the teacher table during small groups. Extending whole group = more off-task behavior, not more learning.

Brain break (2 min) → seat work with visible timer (8 min) → groups.
SAY: "Timer starts now — 8 minutes. Finish the page. When you are done — flip it face down, both hands flat on the table. That tells me you are finished without a word."
⭐ TIMER CONSEQUENCE: Timer goes off. Student not finished. Quiet private conversation: "What happened? Were you focused?" Pattern of not finishing = accountability slip home that night. Consistent every time. That is what makes it real.

2 rotations × 20 minutes — 4 groups of approximately 5-6 students.
Teacher table: Students bring seat work. Work through problems together with manipulatives — linking cubes, counters, barrel of monkeys. One problem at a time. "Show me with the cubes first — then write the answer." If group finishes early — play the center game for this unit.

Adult 2 table: Same worksheet, same manipulatives, same quality of instruction.

Math shelf: Independent game for current unit. Students know the routine. Finish early = When I'm Done chart.

SuccessMaker: Log in. Headphones on. Work independently. Teacher table is not to be interrupted unless it is an emergency.
🧰 NON-NEGOTIABLE: Math must be complete before the paraprofessional goes to lunch — approximately 11:45. Cut seat work short if needed. Do not let math bleed into shared reading or writing time.

Model the writing skill explicitly — then students practice in writing folders.
SAY: "Writers — eyes on me. Today we are working on labeling — label a complete scene with 3 or more labels. Watch me do it first on our chart paper." [Think aloud as you write. Narrate every decision.] "Did you see what I did? Open your writing folder. Try it. I will come around to talk to you about your writing. You have 15 minutes."
Conference with at least 3 students per writing block. One question: "Tell me about your writing. What are you working on?" One suggestion. Move on.

Teacher's Read Aloud · After Lunch · Every Day · Non-Negotiable
1:30 · Bathroom train first → bumblebee spots → read · Joy only · No questions · No comprehension check
~10 min
🚻
Bathroom train immediately when students return. Every student goes. Before anything else.
⭐ BATHROOM TRAIN: Line up. First person in, comes out, taps the next person. No talking. No saving spots. Everyone goes before the book — no interruptions during the read aloud.
📚
Bumblebee spots. Read aloud. No questions. No discussion. Just read.
SAY: "Sit on your spots. Get comfortable. I am going to read to you. All you have to do is listen."
Read the whole book. When it is over — take a breath. Move to guided reading. The book does not need discussion to have impact.

Apple Day Schedule

Read Aloud 1: Apple Star Adventure
SAY: I am going to cut this apple in a special way. Before I do, what do you think we will see inside? Take predictions. Cut horizontally to reveal the star of seeds inside. That star is made of seeds. Every apple has one.
Apple Star Adventure Read Aloud


Apple Tasting Graph: K.MD.B.3
NJ: K.MD.B.3 Classify objects into given categories
SAY: We are going to taste three kinds of apples. Red. Yellow. Green. After you taste all three, put your name on the graph for your favorite color apple. Then we will count and see which color won.
ROUTINE: This graph meets K.MD.B.3. Real math on Apple Day. Count results together. Which has more? Which has fewer? Are any the same?


Read Aloud 2: Color-Along Apple Book
Students color along as you read. Keep it light and fun. This is the regulation activity after the excitement of tasting.

Apple Print Craft
Students use apple halves to stamp prints onto paper or cards. Add stem and leaf with marker when dry.
TIP: Apple print is messy. Cover tables first. Have wet wipes ready. Call students in groups of 4-5 at a time to the craft station while the rest do free choice. This keeps the chaos manageable.

Afternoon: Light and Free
After lunch: read alouds, free choice centers, quiet coloring. No new curriculum. Kids are happy and a little tired. Let them enjoy the afternoon.

Yellow: Afternoon is free choice and light activity. No new content.
Class Dojo Tonight
Today was Apple Day! We tasted red, yellow, and green apples and graphed our class favorites. Ask your child which apple won the class vote. We also made apple print art. What a fun day!

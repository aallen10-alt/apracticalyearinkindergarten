Block 2 NEW GREETING DAY
Morning Meeting Introduce New Greeting
9:40 18 min
1
NEW GREETING: Clap Out Syllables Greeting
SAY: Today is Friday which means we get a brand new greeting we will use all next week. I say your name. You clap the syllables. Ma-ry. [clap clap] E-mi-ly. [clap clap clap] Sam. [clap]
ROUTINE: This greeting Clap Syllables rolls out Mon/Wed/Fri NEXT week. Tue/Thu next week keep Roll the Ball. The week after a new greeting is introduced on Friday again.
Connection: Clapping syllables in names is also the phonological awareness skill from bumblebee spots. Students practice the same skill in two different contexts. Intentional repetition disguised as variety.

Group Share: Weekend Pantomime (acting curriculum)
SAY: Tell your partner one thing you will do this weekend. But the challenge is no words. Just show them with your body.
TIP: Pantomime. Communicating without words. Foundational physical storytelling skill. They think they are playing. They are. And they are learning to use their bodies as expressive instruments.

Administer test at table spots
Red: Read all test directions aloud. Walk the room and observe without helping. Note students who cannot complete any items. These are your Tier 2 candidates.
TIP: Students who shut down during testing: sit next to them quietly. Point to the first item. Say nothing else. Proximity and calm is the intervention.

Mini lesson: what makes a drawing detailed?

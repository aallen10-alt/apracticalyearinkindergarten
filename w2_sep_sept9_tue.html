Phonics Lesson — Letter t — /t/ sound · tap-tip-top-tan-ten · slides + whiteboards
Alphablocks →
SAY: "Hold up the letter T card. 'This is the letter T. It makes the /t/ sound. Put the tip of your tongue behind your top teeth and pop it — /t/. Feel that pop? That is the T sound. It is a quick sound — /t/. Not tuh — just /t/. Say it with me — /t/. /t/. /t/.' Point to picture. 'The picture is a top. Top starts with /t/. What's the first sound in TOP? /t/. Good.'"
Today's words — say each one, tap the sounds, read together:
TAPTIPTOPTANTENTOTTABTIN
SAY: "'Today is whiteboard day. Pick up your marker — cap on the back end. Show me your marker is ready. Now I am going to say a word. You write the first letter only. Just the first letter. Ready? TAP. What is the first sound in TAP? /t/. Write the letter T on your whiteboard. Hold it up. [scan] Good. Erase with your finger. Next word — TIP. First sound? /t/. Write it. Hold it up.' Do all 8 words — first letter only for first 4, then try full CVC for last 4 with support."
🧰 WAIT TIME: After you ask a question — count silently to 10 before calling on anyone. 10 seconds feels long. It is the difference between 3 kids answering and 22 kids thinking.

Snap Word — "see" — introduce and add to the snap word wall.
SAY: "Snap fingers. 'Snap word! The word is SEE. S-E-E. Three letters. Spell it with me — S, E, E. Write it on your whiteboard. Show me. Good — erase it. Write it again without looking. SEE. This word means to look at something. I see you. I see the board. I see my friends.' Add to snap word wall. Count: 'We have two snap words now — I and SEE.'"
Snap words so far: I, see — count the words on the wall together.

Math · Unit 1 Lesson 6 · Count to 5 — count objects in different arrangements · same number different positions
Hook → mini lesson → seat work → 2 rotations
~75 min
K.CC.B.4 K.CC.B.4b
Objective
Count to 5 — count objects in different arrangements · same number different positions
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 minutes — spark curiosity — do NOT teach yet.
SAY: "Put 4 cubes in a straight line. 'How many cubes?' Students count — 4. Rearrange the same 4 cubes into a square shape. 'Now how many?' Wait for responses. 'Did the number change? Let's find out at smartboard spots.'"
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson — smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — manipulatives — word for word script.
SAY: "'Four cubes in a line — let's count: 1, 2, 3, 4. Four. Now I move them.' [Arrange in a square] 'Same cubes — different arrangement. Let's count: 1, 2, 3, 4. Still four! The number of objects does NOT change when you move them around. Whether they're in a line, a circle, or a pile — the number is the same.' Do 3 more arrangements with 5 cubes. Students arrange their own cubes in different ways and count each time to confirm the number stays the same."
🧰 8 MINUTE RULE: Hard cap on whole group instruction. Everything else happens at the small group table. Extending whole group = more off-task behavior, not more learning.

Writing · Unit 1 · Drawing to tell a story — draw a sequence · what happened first, next, last?
Mini lesson 5 min → independent practice 15 min → share
~20 min
W.K.3SL.K.4SL.K.5
Objective
Drawing to tell a story — draw a sequence · what happened first, next, last?
🖊️
Model on chart paper first — then students write independently.
SAY: "'Today we are going to tell a story that has a beginning, a middle, and an end. Watch me.' [Fold paper into thirds] 'First box — what happened first? I draw that here.' [Draw] 'Second box — what happened next? I draw that here.' [Draw] 'Third box — what happened last? I draw that here.' [Draw] 'My drawing tells a whole story — beginning, middle, end. You try. Think of something that happened to you. What was first? Draw it. Then next. Then last. Three boxes. Three moments.'"
Conference with 3 students minimum. One question only: "Tell me about your drawing." One suggestion. Move on.

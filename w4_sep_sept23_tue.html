Phonics — Short a vowel — /ă/ sound · cat-hat-bat-sat-mat · slides + whiteboards
Alphablocks →
SAY: "Hold up the vowel card A. 'We have a special letter today — it is a vowel. Vowels are different from consonants. Every single CVC word has a vowel in the MIDDLE. The vowel A makes two sounds — today we learn the SHORT sound: /ă/. Like this — open your mouth wide: /ă/. That is the short A. CAT, HAT, BAT — all have the short A in the middle. Say /ă/ with me.'"
Today's words — tap sounds, then read:
CATHATBATSATMATRATFATVAN
SAY: "'Whiteboard day. I say a word — you write the MIDDLE letter only. Just the middle sound. Ready? CAT — what is the middle sound? /ă/ — write A. Show me. [scan] Erase. HAT — middle sound? /ă/ — write A. Show me. Erase. BAT — middle? /ă/ — write A.' Do all 8 words — middle letter only. Then: 'Now try the whole word — I say it, you write all three letters. CAT: /k/ = C, /ă/ = A, /t/ = T. Write C-A-T. Show me.' Do HAT and BAT as full words."
🧰 WAIT TIME: Ask the question. Count silently to 10. Then call on someone. 10 seconds. Every time.

Math · Unit 1 Lesson 16 · Compare groups — order 3 numbers from least to greatest
Hook → mini lesson → seat work → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.CC.C.6 K.CC.C.7
Objective
Compare groups — order 3 numbers from least to greatest
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 min — spark curiosity — do NOT teach yet.
SAY: "Put three number cards on the rug — 4, 9, 2 — in random order. 'These are not in order. Before smartboard spots — can you figure out which one is the smallest? Which is the biggest? Think before you touch.'"
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson — smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — word for word.
SAY: "'Ordering numbers means putting them from LEAST to GREATEST — smallest to biggest. We have 4, 9, and 2.' [Hold up each card] '2 is the smallest — it goes first. 4 is the middle — it goes next. 9 is the biggest — it goes last. Least to greatest: 2, 4, 9.' Model with cubes — build a group of 2, a group of 4, a group of 9 and line them up shortest to tallest. 'The towers show you the order — shortest tower is the least.' Students order 3 number cards on their recording sheet and write them least to greatest."
🧰 8 MINUTE RULE: Hard cap. Everything else happens at small group table. Extended whole group = more behavior problems.

Writing · Unit 2 · Labeling Unit begins — what is a label? Draw something and label it with a word
Mini lesson 5 min → independent 15 min
~20 min
W.K.2W.K.3L.K.1L.K.2
Objective
Labeling Unit begins — what is a label? Draw something and label it with a word
🖊️
Model on chart paper first. Then students write in folders.
SAY: "'We are starting a new writing unit today — LABELING. A label is a word that tells readers what something is. Labels use arrows to point at the thing they are naming.' [Draw a classroom scene — door, window, rug, books] 'I am going to label this drawing. Door.' [Write DOOR, draw arrow] 'Window.' [Write WINDOW, draw arrow] 'Rug.' [Write RUG] 'Now you try. Draw a place you know — your bedroom, the cafeteria, the playground. Label as many things as you can. Each label needs a word and an arrow.'"
Conference with 3 students minimum. One question: "Tell me about your writing." One suggestion. Move on.

Phonics — Letter c — /k/ sound · cat-cap-cut-cob-cup · slides + whiteboards
Alphablocks →
SAY: "Hold up the letter C card. 'This is the letter C. Most of the time it makes the /k/ sound — like the letter K. Put the back of your tongue on the roof of your mouth and pop it — /k/. That is the C sound. /k/. /k/. /k/. Say it with me.' Point to picture. 'The picture is a cat. Cat starts with C — and C makes the /k/ sound. CAT. First sound? /k/.'"
Today's words — tap sounds, then read:
CATCAPCUTCOBCUPCABCOTCUB
SAY: "'Whiteboard day — markers ready. I say a word. You write only the first letter on your whiteboard. Just the first letter. Ready? CAT — first sound? /k/ — write the letter C. Hold up. [scan] Erase. CUP — first sound? /k/ — write C. Hold up. [scan] Erase. Now try the whole word — I will help. COB: /k/ write C — /ŏ/ write O — /b/ write B. Show me COB.' Do 4 full words with support."
🧰 WAIT TIME: Ask the question. Count silently to 10. Then call on someone. 10 seconds. Every time.
⚡
Snap Word — "and" — A — N — D — introduce and add to the wall.
SAY: "Snap fingers. 'Snap word review — AND. Write it on your whiteboard right now without looking at the wall. A-N-D. Show me. [scan] Good. Now read the whole wall with me: I — SEE — A — THE — AND.' Students read snap word wall together."
Snap words so far: I, see, a, the, and

Math · Unit 1 Lesson 11 · Count to 10 — say and write numbers 6-10 · number formation 6 7 8 9 10
Hook → mini lesson → seat work → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.CC.A.3
Objective
Count to 10 — say and write numbers 6-10 · number formation 6 7 8 9 10
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 min — spark curiosity — do NOT teach yet.
SAY: "Hold up the numeral card 8. 'What number is this? Raise your hand if you know. How do you write it? Can you write it on your whiteboard right now without help?' Students attempt. Hold up your board. 'Today we practice writing 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.'"
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson — smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — word for word.
SAY: "'Writing numbers 6 through 10. Watch and air-write with me. 6 — curl in, close the circle at the bottom. 6.' [Air write] '7 — across the top, slant down to the right. Some people put a line through the middle — either way is fine. 7.' [Air write] '8 — make an S, close the top. Like two circles stacked. 8.' [Air write] '9 — make a circle at the top, come straight down. 9.' [Air write] '10 — write a 1 and then a 0. Two separate digits. 10.' [Air write] Students practice on their recording sheets — 3 rows of each number 6-10."
🧰 8 MINUTE RULE: Hard cap. Everything else happens at small group table. Extended whole group = more behavior problems.

~20 min

Objective
Drawing to tell a story — label two things in your drawing today
🖊️
Model on chart paper first. Then students write in folders.
SAY: "'Yesterday you labeled one thing in your drawing — just the first letter. Today we label TWO things. And today we stretch the word out a little more. Watch me.' [Draw a sun and a cloud] 'I want to label SUN. I say it slowly — /s/ /ŭ/ /n/. I write what I hear: S-U-N. Arrow to the sun. Now CLOUD. I say it slowly — /k/ /l/ /ow/ /d/. I write what I hear — some letters I know, some I guess. CLD is fine. Arrow to the cloud. Now you try — two labels, two arrows, stretch out the sounds.'"
Conference with 3 students minimum. One question: "Tell me about your writing." One suggestion. Move on.

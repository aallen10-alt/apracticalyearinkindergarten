Structured Literacy Module 1 Lesson 6 Packet Day

Objective
Wednesday is packet day. No slides. Students work through packet pages as a class together.
1
Bumblebee spots: Clap Syllables
SAY: Clap out the syllables with me. Kindergarten. [kin-der-gar-ten] four claps. Apple. [ap-ple] two claps. Sun. one clap.
Kiboomers Syllable Song
2
Letter Sound Cards 3 min
Flash cards rapid fire. Choral response. Keep pace fast.
3
Packet work together
ROUTINE: Open your packet to page blank. Put your finger on the first picture. We do it together. Timer 20 minutes. Do as many pages as you can. Unfinished pages go home.
TIP: Walk the room during packet work. Point to the letter, tap the picture, ask what sound does this make. Let them arrive at the answer.

Block 5 Wednesday Writing Day
Writing Unit 1 Lesson 3 Draw a Person
11:55 30 min
NJ: W.K.2, SL.K.5
Anchor Chart Build With Students
How to Draw a Person
  • Step 1: Circle for the HEAD
  • Step 2: Rectangle for the BODY
  • Step 3: Two lines for LEGS
  • Step 4: Two lines for ARMS
  • Step 5: Add a FACE
  • Step 6: Add HAIR and CLOTHES
Draw every step slowly while talking aloud. This IS the lesson.
1
Mini lesson 10 min on rug
SAY: Watch my hand. Step one. Circle for the head. Air draw with me. Make a circle in the air. Good.
2
Independent drawing 15 min
Circulate. Confer with 4-5 students. Who is this person? Tell me about them.
TIP: There is no wrong person. Yours looks exactly like YOUR person. Draw a deliberately imperfect person and celebrate it.

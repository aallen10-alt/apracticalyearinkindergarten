Frequently Asked Questions
Is this aligned to NJ standards?
Yes. Every lesson includes NJ standard codes for language arts, math, and social studies. The framework also works for most state standards — the skills are the same, the codes may differ slightly.
What curriculum does this use?
The framework is built around structured literacy, conceptual math, and a writers workshop model. It references widely-used curriculum types without being locked to any specific publisher. You adapt it to what you have.
How is content delivered?
Everything lives on this website. You log in, click your month, click your week, click your day. That's it. No downloads, no PDFs, no apps. Works on any device.
When does new content drop?
September is live now. October through June is added monthly. You'll get an email notification the moment a new month is available.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly members can cancel at any time with no penalty. Lifetime members own their access forever.
Is this for public or private school teachers?
Both. The content is built around public school pacing and standards but works beautifully in any kindergarten classroom.

Sheep timer goes off → students at bumblebee spots → greeting → Activity 1 → Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson.
⭐ TUESDAY — WHITEBOARDS: Helper of the day passes whiteboards. Helper picks one friend to pass markers. Wait until every student has both before continuing. 'Pick up your marker. Take the cap off — put the cap on the back end so it doesn't get cold. Show me your marker is ready.'
⭐ MORNING MEETING ORDER — ALWAYS: Sheep timer → bumblebee spots → greeting → PA Activity 1 → PA Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson → brain break → math. Same every day. Students know what comes next. That is the behavior management.
🎵
Activity 1 on bumblebee spots — phonological awareness warm-up — 2 minutes.
SAY: "I am going to say a word. Tell me only the first sound — not the whole word. Just the first sound. Ready? [Say 6-8 words — mostly words starting with today's target sound, 2 distractors with different sounds.] Thumbs up if you hear the Short i vo sound at the beginning. Thumbs down if you don't."
🎵
Activity 2 on bumblebee spots — blending and segmenting — 2 minutes.
SAY: "Now I say the sounds — you blend them into a word. Ready? [Say onset and rime or individual phonemes for 4-5 CVC words.] Now reverse — I say the word, you tap out the sounds on your fingers and tell me how many sounds you hear."
📋
Sing to smartboard spots → read morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson.
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!" Song is the signal — not your voice.
MORNING MESSAGE: "Today is Tuesday. We will use whiteboards. Our snap word is [do]. Look at our schedule." [Point to every block on the schedule. Name each one out loud.]

Letter sound cards — 3 minutes — every card — every single day — non-negotiable even on test days.

🧰 TOOLBOX — WAIT TIME: After asking a question — wait a full 10 seconds before calling on anyone or answering yourself. Count silently. 10 seconds of silence feels like an eternity but it is the difference between 3 students answering and 22 students thinking.

Math · Unit 3 Lesson 4 · 3D Shapes — Real-world examples in the classroom · sort by shape name
Hook bumblebee spots → mini lesson smartboard spots → seat work with timer → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.G.A.1 K.G.A.2
Objective
3D Shapes — Real-world examples in the classroom · sort by shape name
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 minutes — spark curiosity — do not teach yet.
SAY: "Stand up. 'Before we sit down — we are going on a 60-second classroom shape hunt. Find ONE 3D shape in this room. Touch it. Remember what it is. Ready? Go.' Count slowly to 60. 'Come to smartboard spots and tell me what you found.'"
🎵 SING to smartboard spots: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson on smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — manipulatives only — original script — no alien videos.
SAY: "Students share what they found and name the shape. 'Tell me what you found and what 3D shape it is. [student: the globe] Is the globe a sphere? Let's check — is it round all the way around? Yes — sphere. Good. [student: the tissue box] Is that a cube? Let's check — does it have 6 flat sides all the same size? Hmm — the sides might not all be the same. That might be a rectangular prism — like a rectangle that has thickness. Very observant.' Work through 6-8 student responses. 'Now I know you can find 3D shapes with your eyes open. Close your eyes.' [hand students a shape manipulative] 'Touch it. Tell me what shape it is without looking.'"
🧰 TOOLBOX: 8 minutes is the hard cap for whole-group instruction. If you need more time — take it at the teacher table during small groups. Extending whole group = more off-task behavior, not more learning.

Brain break (2 min) → seat work with visible timer (8 min) → groups.
SAY: "Timer starts now — 8 minutes. Finish the page. When you are done — flip it face down, both hands flat on the table. That tells me you are finished without a word."
⭐ TIMER CONSEQUENCE: Timer goes off. Student not finished. Quiet private conversation: "What happened? Were you focused?" Pattern of not finishing = accountability slip home that night. Consistent every time. That is what makes it real.

2 rotations × 20 minutes — 4 groups of approximately 5-6 students.
Teacher table: Students bring seat work. Work through problems together with manipulatives — linking cubes, counters, barrel of monkeys. One problem at a time. "Show me with the cubes first — then write the answer." If group finishes early — play the center game for this unit.

Adult 2 table: Same worksheet, same manipulatives, same quality of instruction.

Math shelf: Independent game for current unit. Students know the routine. Finish early = When I'm Done chart.

SuccessMaker: Log in. Headphones on. Work independently. Teacher table is not to be interrupted unless it is an emergency.
🧰 NON-NEGOTIABLE: Math must be complete before the paraprofessional goes to lunch — approximately 11:45. Cut seat work short if needed. Do not let math bleed into shared reading or writing time.

Shared Reading · Module 2 Week 2 · Read Aloud + Closer Look + myBook
I Like Myself! · Essential Question: What makes each of us special?
~38 min
RL.K.1RL.K.3RL.K.7SL.K.2
Essential Question
What makes each of us special?
📖
Read I Like Myself! — first read — all the way through without stopping first.
SAY: Hold up I Like Myself! by Karen Beaumont. 'Look at the cover. What do you notice about the girl? What do you think she likes about herself? I want you to listen to the whole book and think about one thing YOU like about yourself.' Read the whole book without stopping. After the last page: 'Turn and tell your partner — what is one thing you genuinely like about yourself? It can be something you can do, something about how you look, or something about the way you treat people. Go.'
🧰 REMINDER: Two days per week — Tuesday Read Aloud + myBook, Thursday Big Book + myBook. That is it. All 9 HMH modules fit this way.
📝
myBook response — students at seats — draw and/or write their response.
SAY: Open your myBook to the I Like Myself page — it has the dog-ear. Name on the top line first. Draw one thing you like about yourself and label it. Use what you know about labeling — draw an arrow and write the first letter, or the whole word if you can. You have 10 minutes.
Circulate. Conference with 2-3 students. Sit down, ask "Tell me about your picture," write their words if they want.

Bathroom train immediately when students return. Every student goes. Before anything else.
⭐ BATHROOM TRAIN: Line up. First person in, comes out, taps the next person. No talking. No saving spots. Everyone goes before the book — no interruptions during the read aloud.

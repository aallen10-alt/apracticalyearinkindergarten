Group Share: Mirror Game (acting curriculum)
SAY: Partner up with the person next to you. One person is the mirror. One person moves slowly. Mirror copies every move exactly. Go slowly.
TIP: This is Meisner-based Presence work. Total attention on your partner, responding truthfully in the moment. Taught in every conservatory in the world. These five year olds are doing it right now.

Block 3 Thursday LETTERBOARD DAY
Structured Literacy Module 1 Lesson 7 Letterboard Day
9:55 35 min
NJ: RF.K.1, RF.K.3
Objective
Students build letter-sound correspondence using magnetic letterboards. No slides today.
1
Bumblebee spots: Onset and Rime
SAY: I say a word in two pieces. You put it together. /c/ ... /at/. What is the word? Cat. /d/ ... /og/. Dog. /s/ ... /un/. Sun.
2
Letter Sound Cards 3 min
Fast and choral. Note who is still missing sounds.
3
Magnetic letterboard work
ROUTINE: Find the letter M. Hold it up. Put it on your board on the left side. Say the sound. /m/. Now find the letter T. Put it next to M. Say both sounds.
TIP: Student cannot find the letter: give them a smaller set of 6-8 letters to search through. The visual search is a separate skill from phonics knowledge.
TIP: Student way ahead: Can you build a CVC word? What sounds do you hear in mat? Find those letters and put them in order. Note this student.

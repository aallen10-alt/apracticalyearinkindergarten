Alphablocks →

Math · Unit 1 Lesson 19 · Count to 10 — number review · count, write, order, compare — all skills
Hook → mini lesson → seat work → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.CC.A.1 K.CC.A.2 K.CC.A.3 K.CC.C.6
Objective
Count to 10 — number review · count, write, order, compare — all skills
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 min — spark curiosity — do NOT teach yet.
SAY: "Hold up 10 fingers. 'We started the year counting on our fingers. How much do you know now? Before smartboard spots — write the numbers 1 through 10 on your paper as fast as you can. Go.'"
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson — smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — word for word.
SAY: "'Let's look at everything we can do with numbers to 10. Count forward: 1-10. Count backward: 10-1. Order three numbers. Compare two groups. Count on from any number.' Do one example of each skill together as a class — quick fire review. Then: 'This is your exit ticket — five questions, one of each skill. Show me what you know.' Students complete review independently."
🧰 8 MINUTE RULE: Hard cap. Everything else happens at small group table. Extended whole group = more behavior problems.

Writing · Unit 2 · Labeling — label a diagram · draw something from science and label every part
Mini lesson 5 min → independent 15 min
~20 min
W.K.2W.K.3L.K.1L.K.2
Objective
Labeling — label a diagram · draw something from science and label every part
🖊️
Model on chart paper first. Then students write in folders.
SAY: "'Scientists label their diagrams. A diagram is a drawing that teaches something. Watch.' [Draw a simple plant — roots, stem, leaf, flower] 'This is a plant diagram. I label every part: roots, stem, leaf, flower — arrows to each one. My diagram teaches you about plants. Today you make a diagram of something in nature. It could be a plant, an animal, a raindrop, the sun — anything from the natural world. Label every part you know.'"
Conference with 3 students minimum. One question: "Tell me about your writing." One suggestion. Move on.

Morning Meeting + Phonics · Module 2 Lesson 3 · NO SLIDES
9:40 · Wednesday = Alphablocks episode + Phonics Packet · Consonant b
~50 min
RF.K.2aRF.K.2bRF.K.3SL.K.1
Objective
Students will isolate phonemes, blend phonemes to form words, and read and write the consonant b.
🐑
Sheep timer goes off → bumblebee spots → Wednesday greeting.
Wednesday greeting: students find a partner on the rug, do a special handshake they invented last week, say "Good morning [name]." Quick. Fun. Then:
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
🎵
Activity 1 + Activity 2 — phonological awareness on bumblebee spots.
Activity 1 — Isolating phonemes: "What is the first sound in the word BAT? /b/. What is the first sound in BUS? /b/. What is the first sound in CAT? /k/ — that's different!"

Activity 2 — Blending phonemes: "/b/…/ĭ/…/g/ — what word? Big. /b/…/ă/…/t/ — what word? Bat."
📋
Smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards.
MORNING MESSAGE (Wednesday): "Today is Wednesday. We will watch Alphablocks and do our packet. Our snap word is [as]. Look at our schedule."
Letter sound cards — 3 minutes — fast and automatic.
📺
Watch Alphablocks — Letter B episode — on smartboard.
Alphablocks Letter B → Students watch on smartboard. No slides today — this IS the phonics instruction for Wednesday. The episode teaches the sound through story, which is more engaging and developmentally appropriate than slides for five year olds.
📄
Phonics packet — set a timer — work through together.
Distribute packets while students are transitioning to seats. Set a visible timer for 20-25 minutes.
SAY: "Open your packet to page 1. Put your finger on the first picture. I'm going to read the directions and we will do the first one together. Then you try the rest."
Work through each page together — model the first item, students complete the rest independently. Circulate. Redirect students who are off task quietly.
⭐ WEDNESDAY ROUTINE: NO SLIDES on Wednesdays. Alphablocks + packet. Always. Students learn to expect this. The packet is the practice. The episode is the instruction.

Math · Unit 2 Lesson 3 · Compare Groups to 10
Hook → teach → seat work with timer → groups → Numberblocks
~75 min
K.CC.C.6K.CC.C.7MP.2
Objective
Students will compare two groups of objects up to 10 and identify which has more, fewer, or the same number.
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — curiosity question — 3 min.
Hold up two bags — one with 7 counters, one with 4 counters.
SAY: "Yesterday we compared groups up to 5. Today I have more counters. Can you still figure out which bag has more without counting? Come to your smartboard spots and we'll test your hypothesis."
📊
Teach on smartboard spots — manipulatives — 8 min.
Line up 7 counters and 4 counters in matching pairs. Same strategy as yesterday — leftovers = MORE.
SAY: "Same strategy. Line them up. Count the leftovers. 7 has 3 leftovers. 4 has none. So 7 is MORE than 4. 4 is FEWER than 7. Say it with me — 7 is MORE. 4 is FEWER."
One more example: 9 vs 6. Students call it out. Then one example where both groups are the same — what do we call that? SAME. Add to anchor chart. Numberblocks 1-10 Compare →

Writing · Labeling Unit · Lesson 2
Writers add labels to more than one part of their drawing
~20 min
W.K.2L.K.1L.K.2
Objective
Students will add two labels to a drawing — an arrow and the first letter of each labeled part.
🖊️
Mini lesson — 5 min — model two labels on one drawing.
Hold up yesterday's modeled drawing — it had one label. Add a second label today.
SAY: "Yesterday we added one label. Today we are going to add two. Watch me — I'll add a label for the arm." [draw arrow, write A] "Now I have two labels. Today you are going to try two labels on your picture."

Social Studies · Community Helpers · Lesson 2
3:20–3:30 · Who helps in our community? · Firefighters and doctors
~10 min
SS.K.C.1SS.K.CC.1
🏛️
Community Helpers — Lesson 2 — Firefighters and Doctors.
Show pictures of a firefighter and a doctor. Ask: "What does a firefighter do? How do they help us? What does a doctor do?"
SAY: "Community helpers are people whose job is to help other people. Today we are looking at two — firefighters and doctors. Turn and tell your partner: which community helper would you want to be and why?"
Take 3-4 responses. Quick draw — students draw themselves as a community helper on a half sheet. Hold up and share.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Social studies lives in the 3:20 block — 10 minutes. Keep it moving. The draw-and-share activity takes 5 minutes and gives every student a chance to speak. Do not let it run long.

Morning Meeting + Phonics · Module 2 Lesson 2
9:40 · Tuesday = Slides + Whiteboards · Consonant s · Snap Word: as
~50 min
RF.K.2aRF.K.2b RF.K.3SL.K.1L.K.1
Objective
Students will blend onset and rime to form words, identify rhyming word pairs, and read and write the consonant s and the snap word "as."
🐑
Sheep timer goes off → students already at bumblebee spots → greeting.
Tuesday greeting: students go to smartboard spots. Greet the person next to them with a handshake and say their name. Teach them: firm handshake, eye contact, say their name clearly. Practice once together first.
SAY: "Good morning everyone. It is Tuesday. Today we use whiteboards. When I say go, shake hands with the person next to you and say their name. Ready? Go."
🎵
Activity 1 on bumblebee spots — phonological awareness warm-up.
Rhyming: say two words, students give thumbs up for rhyme, thumbs down for no rhyme.
SAY: "I'm going to say two words. If they rhyme — thumbs up. If they don't — thumbs down. Ready? Sun — fun." [pause] "Sat — hat." [pause] "Dog — fog." [pause] "Cat — cup."
🎵
Activity 2 on bumblebee spots — blending onset and rime.
SAY: "I'm going to say the beginning of a word and the end. You put them together. /s/…/un/. What word? Sun. /s/…/at/. What word? Sat. /s/…/it/. What word? Sit."
Alphablocks Letter S →
📋
Move to smartboard spots → read the morning message together.
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
Read the morning message together. Point to each word as you read.
MORNING MESSAGE (Tuesday — reusable all month): "Today is Tuesday. We will use our whiteboards. Our snap word is [as]. Look at our schedule."
Point to the daily schedule. Name each block. Students know what's coming — this is the behavior management trigger that keeps the day running smoothly.
🃏
Letter sound cards — at smartboard spots — 3 minutes.
Hold up each card one at a time. Students call out the sound. No singing, no games — just fast and automatic. This is daily non-negotiable practice.
⭐ ROUTINE: Letter sound cards happen every single day at smartboard spots after the morning message. Always. Takes 3 minutes. Non-negotiable even on crazy days.
🔴
Phonics Lesson — Consonant s — Slides + Whiteboards.
Pull up slides. Show the letter S card.
SAY: "This is the letter S. It makes the /s/ sound. Say it with me — /s/. Now — [student name], can you be our whiteboard helper today? Please pass the whiteboards to each friend. Pick someone to pass the markers."
Wait for whiteboards to be distributed. Then:
SAY: "Pick up your marker. Take the cap off — put the cap on the back end so it doesn't get cold. Show me your marker is ready."
Now teach:
SAY: "Show me capital S on your whiteboard. Watch me first." [write it on the board] "Now you try. Capital S. Show me." [scan the room] "Good — erase it with your finger. Now lowercase s. Watch me." [write it] "Your turn. Lowercase s. Show me."
Tap out CVC words with s: sit, set, sun, sob, sap. For each word:
SAY: "New word — SIT. Tap the sounds with me on your fingers — /s/ /i/ /t/. Three sounds. Now write SIT on your whiteboard. Show me when you're done."
🧰 TOOLBOX: Students who can't hold the marker correctly — hand-over-fist grip is fine right now. The goal is getting sounds on paper, not perfect penmanship. Fix grip quietly during guided reading pulls, not during whole group.
🧰 TOOLBOX — ASKING FOR HELP: If a student is stuck, they need to say specifically what they need: "I don't understand how to write the letter s" — not "I don't get it." Teach this language explicitly. You cannot help a student who can't tell you what they need.
⚡
Snap Word — "as" — introduce at smartboard spots.
SAY: "This is a snap word." [snap fingers] "You know it in a snap — just like that. The word is AS. Spell it with me — A, S. Write AS on your whiteboard." [wait] "Show me." [scan] "Now write it in the air." [do it together] "Now cap your markers — put the hat back on — and put your whiteboard on the floor in front of you."
Add AS to the snap word wall.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Snap words cannot be sounded out phonetically — that's why they're snap words. Don't let students try to decode them. Just learn them by sight, by feel, in a snap.
🧠
Brain break — 2 minutes — then into math.
Today's embedded brain break: Freeze like a statue in a silly shape. Hold it for 5 seconds. Then freeze in a different shape. Repeat 3 times. Students practice controlling their bodies — they don't know they're learning performance skills. GoNoodle →

Math · Unit 2 Lesson 2 · Compare Groups to 5
Hook on bumblebee spots → teach on smartboard spots → seat work with timer → groups
~75 min
K.CC.C.6K.CC.B.5MP.2MP.6
Objective
Students will compare two groups of objects up to 5 and identify which has more, fewer, or the same number.
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 minutes — curiosity only.
Hold up 3 red counters in one hand and 5 blue counters in the other.
SAY: "I have counters in both hands. Don't tell me the answer yet — just think. Which hand has more? Come to your smartboard spots and we'll find out."
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📊
Teach on smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — manipulatives not video.
Put 3 red counters in a line and 5 blue counters in a line next to it on the carpet where everyone can see.
SAY: "Count the red ones with me. 1, 2, 3. Count the blue ones. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Which group has more? Watch what happens when I line them up side by side." [line them up in matching pairs] "See how the blue ones have some left over? Those leftovers — that's MORE. The side with no leftovers — that's FEWER."
New vocabulary — add to anchor chart: MORE · FEWER · SAME. Students repeat each word.

Two more practice examples together using counters. Students call out MORE or FEWER. Numberblocks Comparing →
⏱️
Seat work — set the timer visibly — 8 minutes.
Students go to seats. Math worksheet is already there. Set a visible timer.
SAY: "When the timer goes off, pencils down. Whatever you finished is what you finished. If you need to keep working, that happens at the teacher table during group time."
Circulate for 3 minutes. Then begin pulling groups.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Student did not finish when timer went off — quiet conversation, not public. If it becomes a pattern, the accountability system kicks in. One time is not a pattern.

Shared Reading · Module 2 Week 1 · Tuesday
I Am René the Boy · Read Aloud + Closer Look + myBook response page
~38 min
RL.K.1RL.K.3 RL.K.7SL.K.2L.K.6
Essential Question — Module 2
What makes each of us special? Students identify key details and connect the story to their own experience of what makes them unique.
📖
Read I Am René the Boy — first read — all the way through without stopping.
Hold the book so every student can see. Read it. Let them experience it first without questions.
AFTER READING — SAY: "René is special in so many ways. I'm going to ask you one question — just one. Think about it. What is one thing that makes YOU special? Not René — you. Turn and tell your partner."
Take 3-4 responses. Move on. Do not over-question.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Tuesday = Read Aloud book + closer look + myBook. That is it. Do not attempt all five program lessons. Two days per week, two books — all nine modules fit in the school year.
📝
myBook response — students go to seats — draw one thing that makes them special.
SAY: "Open your myBook. Find the page about René — it has the dog-ear. Put your name on the line at the top first. Then draw one thing that makes YOU special — just like René. Just a picture is fine."
Circulate. Conference with 3-4 students. Ask: "Tell me about your picture." Write their words at the bottom if they want.

Writing · Labeling Unit · Lesson 1
What is a label? Writers name what they drew.
~20 min
W.K.2L.K.1L.K.2
Objective
Students will understand that labels name the parts of a drawing. Students will add one label — one letter — to their own picture using beginning sounds.
🖊️
Mini lesson — 5 minutes — model labeling with an arrow.
Hold up a drawing of a person with one label — "H" with an arrow pointing to the head.
SAY: "Writers do not just draw — they label. A label is a word that tells us what something is. I drew a person and I labeled the head. See the arrow? The arrow points to what the word names. Today you are going to add one label to your drawing. Just the first letter — that is enough for today."
Model: draw something simple, draw an arrow, write the first letter only.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Lesson 1 = first letter only. A student who writes "H" with an arrow pointing to a head is doing exactly what they should. Celebrate it. Do not push for more than one letter today.
✏️
Independent practice — 15 minutes — students label one part of their drawing.
Students take out writing folders. Pick a drawing from the drawing unit OR start a new one. Add one label — arrow and one letter.

Conference with 3-4 students. Ask: "What is this?" "What sound does that start with?" "Can you write that letter next to your arrow?"

~10 min

Bathroom train first — then bumblebee spots — then read.
⭐ ROUTINE: Back from lunch — bathroom train starts immediately. Students tap the next person in the chain. Go, come back, tap. Then bumblebee spots. Then read aloud. This sequence is the same every single day.
📚
Read aloud — teacher's choice — joy only — no questions.
SAY: "Sit on your spots. Get comfortable. I am going to read to you. Just listen."
Read it. No comprehension check. No questions. This read aloud is for nervous system regulation — theirs and yours. It signals: the hard part of the day is done. We are safe. We are together.

Social-Emotional Learning · Responsibility · Week 1
3:20–3:30 · Back from specials · 10 minutes · Thumbs Up, Move Up
~10 min
SEL.K.1SEL.K.2
🩷
Responsibility — Thumbs Up, Move Up — following directions game.
SAY: "Responsibility means doing what you are supposed to do — even when no one is watching. Today we practice following directions. If you follow the direction correctly the first time — thumbs up, move up one seat."
Give 3-step directions. Students who follow correctly on the first try — thumbs up, move one seat. Play 3-4 rounds. Keep it fun and fast.
🧰 TOOLBOX: SEL lives in the 3:20-3:30 block. Ten minutes. Do not let it go longer. This is a CONNECT activity today — the formal GROW lesson happens once a week on a different day.

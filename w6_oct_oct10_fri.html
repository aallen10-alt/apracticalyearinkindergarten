Phonics Focus
TEST DAY — Module 2 L1-4 · Consonants s, b, n review · Alphablocks + assessment
⭐ TEST DAY · Alphablocks review episode first · Assessment after · Celebrate every student
🐑
Sheep timer → bumblebee spots → Friday greeting → Activity 1 → Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson → brain break → math.
⭐ ORDER — ALWAYS: Sheep timer → bumblebee spots → greeting → PA Activity 1 → PA Activity 2 → sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson → brain break → math. Same every day. Students knowing what comes next IS the behavior management.
🎵
Activity 1 on bumblebee spots — phonological awareness — 2 minutes.
SAY: "I am going to say a word. Tell me only the first sound. Just the first sound — not the whole word. Ready? [Say 6-8 words — mostly words starting with today's target sound, 2 distractors.] Thumbs up if you hear the /t/ sound at the beginning. Thumbs down if you don't."
🎵
Activity 2 on bumblebee spots — blending and segmenting — 2 minutes.
SAY: "Now I say the sounds separately — you blend them into a word. Ready? [Give 4-5 CVC words in separate phonemes.] Now reverse — I say the word, you tap the sounds on your fingers and tell me how many."
📋
Sing to smartboard spots → morning message → letter sound cards → phonics lesson.
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!" Song is the signal — not your voice. Never your voice.
MORNING MESSAGE: "Today is Friday. We will review and take our assessment. Our snap word is [as]. Look at our schedule." [Point to every block on the posted schedule. Name each one out loud.]
Letter sound cards — 3 minutes — every card — every single day — non-negotiable. Hold up each card. Students call out the sound. No exceptions. Not even on test days. Not even on Halloween.
🔴
Phonics Lesson — TEST DAY — Module 2 L1-4 · Consonants s, b, n review · Alphablocks + assessment
Alphablocks →
Play the Alphablocks review episode. Students watch. Then distribute the assessment. Read each direction aloud. Students work independently. Circulate — do not help with answers — do help with understanding directions.
🧰 WAIT TIME: After asking a question — count silently to 10 before calling on anyone. 10 seconds feels endless. It is the difference between 3 students answering and 22 students thinking.
⚡
Snap Word — "as" — introduce and add to the snap word wall.
SAY: [Snap fingers] "Snap word! The word is AS. Spell it with me — AS. Write it on your paper in the air. Write it again. One more time. We now have these snap words: a, the, as. You know all of them in a snap."
Add to snap word wall. Count the words on the wall together.

Math · Unit 2 Lesson 5 · Compare groups — unit review · apply comparing to real-world word problems
Hook bumblebee spots → mini lesson smartboard spots → seat work timer → 2 rotations × 20 min
~75 min
K.CC.C.6 K.OA.A.2
Objective
Compare groups — unit review · apply comparing to real-world word problems
🔵
Hook on bumblebee spots — 3 minutes — spark curiosity — do NOT teach yet.
SAY: "Tell a story: 'There are 6 birds on a wire and 8 birds in a tree. Which group has more birds? Which group has fewer? How many more birds are in the tree than on the wire?' Pause. 'Come to smartboard spots and we'll solve it together.'"
🎵 SING: "Smartboard spots, smartboard spots, find your smartboard spot!"
📐
Mini lesson on smartboard spots — 8 minutes MAX — manipulatives — original script — no alien videos.
SAY: "'This is a word problem — math that lives in a story. Let's draw it: 6 birds on the wire.' [Draw on board] '8 birds in the tree.' [Draw] 'Which is more? 8. How many more? Count on from 6 to 8: 6... 7, 8. Two more birds in the tree.' Students solve 3 word problems on their assessment page using drawings and numbers."
🧰 8 MINUTE RULE: Hard cap on whole-group instruction. If more time is needed — take it at the teacher table during small groups. Extending whole group = more off-task behavior, not more learning.
Numberblocks →
⏱️
Brain break (2 min) → seat work with visible timer (8 min) → groups.
SAY: "Timer starts now — 8 minutes. Finish your page. When you are done — flip it face down, both hands flat on the table. That tells me you are finished without a word."
⭐ TIMER CONSEQUENCE: Timer goes off. Student not finished. Quiet private conversation: "What happened? Were you focused?" Consistent every time. That is what makes it real.
🎯
2 rotations × 20 minutes — 4 groups of approximately 5-6 students.
Teacher table: Students bring seat work. Work through problems with manipulatives — linking cubes, counters, barrel of monkeys. "Show me with the cubes first — then write the answer."

Adult 2 table: Same worksheet, same manipulatives, same quality of instruction.

Math shelf: Independent game for current unit. Students already know the routine.

SuccessMaker: Log in. Headphones on. Independent.
🧰 NON-NEGOTIABLE: Math must finish before paraprofessional lunch — approximately 11:45. Cut seat work if needed. Math does not bleed into writing or shared reading time.
🧰 ASKING FOR HELP: "I don't understand how to ____." That is the only acceptable form. "I don't get it" gets redirected every single time.

Objective
Sentences — I like ___. Add a describing word · I like big dogs. I like red apples.
🖊️
Model the writing skill on chart paper — then students practice in writing folders.
SAY: "Writers — eyes on me. Watch me do this first." [Think aloud as you write. Narrate every decision: 'I am going to draw first, then add my label, I need to think about what sound I hear first in this word...'] "Did you see what I did? Open your writing folder. Try it. I will come around to talk to you about your writing. You have 15 minutes."
Conference with 3 students minimum. One question: "Tell me about your writing." One suggestion. Move on.

Teacher's Read Aloud · After Lunch · Every Day · Non-Negotiable
1:30 · Bathroom train first → bumblebee spots → read · Joy only · No questions · No comprehension check
~10 min
🚻
Bathroom train immediately when students return. Every student goes before the book.
⭐ BATHROOM TRAIN: Line up. First person in, comes out, taps next person. No talking. No saving spots. Everyone goes. No interruptions during the read aloud.
📚
Bumblebee spots. Read. No questions. No discussion. Just read.
SAY: "Sit on your spots. Get comfortable. I am going to read to you. All you have to do is listen."
Read the whole book. When it is over — take a breath. Move to guided reading. The book does not need discussion to have impact.

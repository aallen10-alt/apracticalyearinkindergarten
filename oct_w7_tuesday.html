Structured Literacy · Module 2 Lesson 2 · Consonant s · Rhyme + Blending
Today: Slides + Whiteboards

Phonological Awareness (3 min):
Rhyme: "I'm going to say two words. Tell me if they rhyme — sun/fun, dog/log, cat/hat, sit/bit." Thumbs up for rhyme, thumbs down for no rhyme.

Blending words: "I'm going to say the beginning and end of a word. You put it together — /s/…/un/ → sun. /s/…/et/ → set."
🎵 ALPHABLOCKS: Alphablocks Letter S →

Phonics Skill — Consonant s (8 min):
Pull up slides. Show the letter S card. Students call out the sound.
SAY: "This is the letter S. What sound does it make? /s/. Say it with me — /s/. Now show me S on your whiteboard. Capital S. Now lowercase s. Good. Erase it."
Tap out CVC words with S: sit, set, sun, sob, sap. Students tap fingers for each sound, then write on whiteboard.
SAY: "I'm going to say a word. Tap out the sounds with me. S-I-T. How many sounds? 3. Write the word on your whiteboard. Show me."
🧰 TOOLBOX: Kids who can't hold the marker correctly — hand over fist grip is fine right now. The goal is getting sounds on paper, not perfect penmanship. Fix grip gently during guided reading pulls.

Snap Word — "as" (3 min):
Hold up the snap word card. "This is a snap word — you know it in a snap, just like that." Snap fingers. "The word is AS. Spell it with me — A, S. Write it on your whiteboard. Now write it in the air. Now write it on your neighbor's back."
🧰 TOOLBOX: Snap words = words that can't be sounded out. Don't try to phonetically decode them. Just learn them by sight, in a snap. Build the snap word wall — add AS today.

Math · Topic 2 Lesson 2
Compare Groups to 5 · Whole Group then Centers
~75 min
K.CC.C.6 K.CC.B.5 MP.2 MP.6
Objective
Students will compare two groups of objects (up to 5) and determine which group has more, fewer, or the same number.
📊
Whole Group Lesson (20 min)
Put 3 red counters and 5 blue counters on the carpet where everyone can see.
SAY: "How many red counters do I have? Count with me. 1, 2, 3. How many blue? 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Which group has more? How do you know?"
Introduce the vocabulary: MORE, FEWER, SAME. Put them on the anchor chart. Students make a line of 3 counters and a line of 5 counters — line them up side by side. The one that sticks out = MORE.
SAY: "When you line them up and one side has leftovers — those leftovers are MORE. The side with no leftovers has FEWER."
Practice 3 more examples together. Students call out MORE or FEWER. Numberblocks Comparing →
🧰 TOOLBOX: Math finishes before paraprofessional goes to lunch (~11:45). Do not go past 11:55. If the lesson is running long, cut the independent practice and go straight to centers.
🎯
Small Group Rotations (2 rotations × 20 min)
Three centers running. Teacher pulls one group at the teacher table.

Center 1 — Math Shelf (independent): Counters, linking cubes, or pattern blocks. Students make two groups and decide which has more.

Center 2 — SuccessMaker (independent): Log in. Complete assigned activities. Headphones on.

Center 3 — Teacher Table: Pull the group that needs the most support. Use physical counters. Ask: "Can you show me 3? Can you show me 5? Which is more?"
🧰 TOOLBOX: Students who finish early go to the "When I'm Done" anchor chart. No exceptions. Do not let them wander. The anchor chart is your behavior management tool during centers.

HMH Shared Reading · Module 2 Week 1
I Am René the Boy / Soy René el Niño · Tuesday = Read Aloud + Closer Look + myBook
~38 min
RL.K.1 RL.K.3 RL.K.7 SL.K.2 L.K.6
Objective · Module 2 Essential Question
What makes each of us special? Students will identify key details in the story and connect the text to their own experiences of what makes them unique.
📖
Read Aloud — I Am René the Boy (first read) + Closer Look
Hold the book so every student can see. Introduce the book.
SAY: "Our new book is called I Am René the Boy. Look at the cover. What do you notice about René? What do you think this book might be about?"
Read the whole book through without stopping. Let them hear it first.

After reading:
SAY: "René is special in a lot of ways. What is one thing that makes René special? Now think — what is one thing that makes YOU special? Turn and tell your partner."
Take 3-4 responses. Keep it moving. Don't over-question.
🧰 TOOLBOX: Amy's way for HMH — Tuesday = Read Aloud book, closer look, myBook. That's it. Do not attempt to do all 5 program lessons. Two days per week, Amy's way, all 9 modules fit.
📝
myBook Response Page
Students go to tables. Open myBook to the Module 2 Week 1 response page. Read the directions together. Students draw and/or write their response.
SAY: "Open your myBook to the dog-ear page. Find the page about René. Today you're going to draw one thing that makes YOU special — just like René. Put your name on the line at the top first."
Circulate. Comment on what students draw. Ask "Tell me about your picture."

Writing · Unit 2: Labeling · Lesson 1
What is a label? Writers tell us what they drew.
~20 min
W.K.2 L.K.1 L.K.2
Objective
Students will understand that labels name the parts of a picture. Students will label one part of their own drawing using beginning sounds.
🖊️
Mini Lesson — What is a Label? (5 min)
Hold up a drawing of a person with one label — "head" — with an arrow pointing to it.
SAY: "Writers don't just draw — they label. A label is a word that tells us what something is. I drew a person and I labeled the head. See the arrow? The arrow points to what the word names. Today you are going to add one label to your drawing."
Model: draw a simple picture, draw an arrow, write the first letter of the word (only the first letter — that's enough for Lesson 1).
🧰 TOOLBOX: Lesson 1 = first letter only. Do not expect whole words yet. A student who writes "H" with an arrow pointing to a head is doing exactly what they should. Praise it.
✏️
Independent Practice (15 min)
Students take out their writing folder. They pick a drawing from Unit 1 OR start a new drawing today. They add ONE label — an arrow and one letter.

Circulate and conference with 3-4 students. Ask: "What is this?" "What sound does that start with?" "Can you write that letter?"
